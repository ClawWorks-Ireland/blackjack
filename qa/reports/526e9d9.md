# QA round 1 — BlackJack 526e9d9 (hand history)

Tester: BlackJack-player · 2026-09-25 · snapshot of commit 526e9d9 (not the live working tree)

## What I ran
- `npm test` in the snapshot: 42/42 pass.
- Engine fuzz (`qa/engine_fuzz.js`): 40,000 rounds of random legal play + 200,000 rounds of basic strategy, every round checked against an independent rules implementation (settlement, dealer S17 draws, insurance, split aces, doubles, money conservation).
  - 0 invariant violations. Basic-strategy return 99.83% (expected ≈99.5% for these rules; within noise). The rules engine is solid.
- Browser playtest (`qa/ui_playtest.js`, headless Chromium): 80 rounds with splits, doubles, surrenders, insurance, blackjacks and the S shortcut; bet validation; reload; going broke and resetting; a 390px phone; full-motion deal animation. No console errors.
